6. How the Algorithm Works

  1. An initiator records its local state.
  2. It sends a marker on each outgoing channel before sending any further application messages on that channel.
  3. A process receiving its first marker records its local state and forwards markers on its outgoing channels.
  4. It records messages arriving on each incoming channel after its local snapshot until that channel's marker arrives.
  5. When all processes have recorded their states and all incoming channels are closed for recording, the global snapshot is complete.

A global snapshot consists of all recorded process states and all recorded in-transit channel messages. It does not require every process to stop executing.
